# Week Choices Table
I designed this table as a personal utility for writing down all work-related choices I am proud of making for the week. As someone who is neurodivergent and with a PDA profile (which stands for Pervasive Drive for Autonomy), traditional week planners can trigger demand resistance and make it very difficult to make any use of them.

## Table Contents
As of writing this, the table couldn't be any more barebones. Simplicity and freedom are my top goals for it.
- I personally use these tables by printing them and writing my choices with a pen.
- The checkboxes on the left exist so I can mark my day as complete, which signals to me I am completely free to relax and de-stress.
- There is no obligation to fill out any of the spaces. If a particular day feels heavier than others, I simply write "Day Off" under the Choices column and check the mark on the left.

If you are a fellow neurodivergent individual with a PDA profile, and you like the idea of trying an autonomy-first approach to your proudest achievements, feel free to clone this repository and print the tables whenever you need them. Alternatively, you can fill them out digitally if you don't have a printer or don't want to commit to printing tables. Sometime in the future, I might try to provide a more app-like experience in the near future where you can create a new folder with tables using an automated script, and then you will have the option to write inside HTML textboxes. Within each folder, a `launch.bat` and `launch.sh` might be included in case your computer doesn't know to open `.html` files using your default browser.
